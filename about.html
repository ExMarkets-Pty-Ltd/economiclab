About ExMarkets

ABOUT EXMARKETS

ExMarkets is a financial news and market information platform focused on delivering clear, accessible and timely coverage of global markets, business, economics and technology.

ExMarkets aims to make financial information easier to understand while providing readers with news, market information, analysis and business coverage in a single professional destination.

Our Mission

Delivering clear financial news and accessible market information

ExMarkets aims to deliver clear financial news, provide accessible market information, cover business and economic developments, highlight important technology and innovation trends, and help readers better understand the forces influencing global markets.

Founder & Owner

Lulama Lupin Makondo, also known as Lupin Khuvutlu, is the Founder and Owner of ExMarkets Group. His vision for ExMarkets is to build a modern financial media platform that connects market information, business news, economic developments and technology in one accessible destination.

Our Editorial Approach

Clarity, accuracy, context and responsible reporting

Clarity
