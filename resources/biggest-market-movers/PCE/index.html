Underlying inflation

Core PCE Inflation

Core PCE excludes food and energy prices and is commonly used to assess underlying inflation trends. The Federal Reserve's official 2% objective remains defined using total PCE inflation.

Federal Reserve context

Why PCE Matters to the Federal Reserve

The Federal Reserve's 2% longer-run inflation objective is expressed in terms of PCE inflation. Persistent inflation above that objective can increase expectations for tighter monetary policy depending on inflation persistence, spending, labor conditions, growth and financial conditions.

How PCE Can Move Financial Markets

US Dollar

Inflation surprises can change policy expectations and potentially affect USD.

Treasury Yields

Gold

Real yields, USD movements and inflation expectations can affect gold.

Equities & Bitcoin

Rate expectations and liquidity may influence valuations and risk appetite.

How Fundamental Traders Watch PCE

At Release

Compare actual with forecast and previous data, then compare headline, core, goods and services.

After Release

Observe USD, yields, gold, Nasdaq, S&P 500, Bitcoin and changing expectations.
