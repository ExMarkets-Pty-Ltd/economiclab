Understanding Dividends

A dividend is a distribution of a company’s earnings to eligible shareholders. Dividend dates help investors understand when ownership must be established and when cash distributions are expected.

Ex-dividend dateThe first date on which a buyer is generally not entitled to the declared dividend.
Record dateThe date the company uses to identify shareholders eligible for the distribution.
Payment dateThe scheduled date on which the dividend is paid to eligible shareholders.

Dividend information is provided for informational and educational purposes only and should not be considered investment, financial or tax advice. Dividend amounts and payment dates may change and should be verified with the relevant company or official market source.
