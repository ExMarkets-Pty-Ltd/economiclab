Market context

How Federal Reserve Decisions Can Affect Markets

US Dollar

Reaction depends on yield differentials, expectations and the guidance already priced in.

Gold

Real yields, the dollar, inflation expectations and risk appetite can all matter.

Treasuries

The curve can respond differently to policy, growth and inflation signals.

Equities & Bitcoin

Valuations and risk sentiment may shift with liquidity and forward expectations.

Market reactions are conditional, not automatic. Positioning, growth outlook, inflation and forward guidance can change the outcome.
