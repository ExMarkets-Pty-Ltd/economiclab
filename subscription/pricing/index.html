What does an EconomicLab subscription provide?

Depending on the plan, a subscription provides access to designated market-event intelligence, XAUUSD signals and analysis, and subscriber market research described on this page.

What is the NFP signal?

It is an EconomicLab signal focused on XAUUSD, delivered five minutes before the US Nonfarm Payrolls event as part of the XAUUSD Signal plan.

No. EconomicLab does not guarantee profits, returns, accuracy or any particular market outcome. Markets involve risk and all decisions remain the user's responsibility.

No. Signals, analysis and commentary are informational and educational content, not financial, investment or other professional advice.
