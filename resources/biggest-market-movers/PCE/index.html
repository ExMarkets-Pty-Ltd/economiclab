Policy context

Why PCE Matters to the Federal Reserve

Headline PCE measures the broadest prices consumers face, while core PCE helps strip out volatile food and energy moves. Persistent inflation above the Federal Reserve's objective can increase expectations for tighter policy, depending on labor-market conditions, spending, inflation expectations and the broader outlook.

Educational framework

How Fundamental Traders Watch PCE

Before the Release

Monitor previous PCE, core PCE, CPI, wages, spending and Fed expectations.

At Release

Compare actual versus forecast and previous readings, then review headline versus core and goods versus services.

After Release

Watch USD, yields, gold, equities and rate expectations without assuming a fixed reaction.
