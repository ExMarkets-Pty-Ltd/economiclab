Earnings

Track the earnings events that move markets.

The EconomicLab Earnings Calendar helps investors and market participants monitor scheduled corporate earnings releases and identify reporting events that may influence individual stocks, sectors and broader financial markets.

Use the calendar to track upcoming earnings announcements, compare reporting schedules and prepare for periods when new financial results may materially affect market expectations.

Earnings Calendar

Track scheduled earnings announcements from publicly listed companies and monitor reporting periods across major global markets.

Earnings releases can provide important information about a company's revenue, profitability, guidance, operating performance and outlook. They can also influence investor expectations, share prices, sector valuations and broader market sentiment.

The EconomicLab Earnings Calendar is designed to provide a clear reference point for upcoming reporting events and support market research and preparation.

Research focus

What to monitor during earnings season

Revenue and earnings

Monitor reported revenue and earnings performance against previous periods and market expectations.

Earnings per share

Review reported earnings per share and how results compare with analyst expectations where consensus estimates are available.

Company guidance

Pay attention to management's forward guidance and changes to expectations for future revenue, earnings and business performance.

Sector performance

Compare individual company results with broader sector trends to identify whether earnings developments are company-specific or part of a wider market theme.

Market reaction

Monitor how investors respond to earnings results, particularly when the reported numbers or management outlook differ materially from expectations.

Market context

Why earnings matter

Earnings announcements can be important market-moving events because they provide new information about a company's financial performance and future outlook.

Significant differences between actual results, expectations and forward guidance can contribute to increased volatility in individual securities and related sectors.

An earnings release does not guarantee a particular market reaction. Prices can move in either direction depending on expectations, positioning and broader market conditions.
