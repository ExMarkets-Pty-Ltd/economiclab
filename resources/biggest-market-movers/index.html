Market context

Why These Events Matter

Scheduled data and central-bank decisions can reshape expectations for growth, inflation, interest rates and liquidity. That makes them important reference points for market research and trade planning.

The effect is not limited to one asset class. Policy and macroeconomic developments can flow through currencies, bonds, equities, commodities, gold, risk sentiment and broader financial conditions.
