What EconomicLab Provides

A clearer view of economic and market context

Market Intelligence

Coverage across major financial markets

EconomicLab brings together market information and analysis across forex, commodities, indices, cryptocurrencies, equities and broader market movements.

Economic Intelligence

Macroeconomic context that helps explain market moves

The platform connects users to inflation, GDP, employment, interest-rate and central-bank developments through official data and editorial analysis.

Company Intelligence

Business context for the companies shaping markets

EconomicLab is building a stronger connection between company developments, earnings activity, sector context and wider market themes.

Research & Insights

Analysis designed to explain what happened and why it matters

Through the EconomicLab insights model, users can follow market events, understand their economic and business context, and evaluate the implications for markets.

Data & Analytics
