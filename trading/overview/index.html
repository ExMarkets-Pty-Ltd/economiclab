Market coverage

Markets move for different reasons.

Financial markets respond to a combination of economic data, monetary policy, corporate developments, geopolitical events, investor sentiment and changes in market expectations. Understanding these drivers can help traders place individual price movements within a broader market context.

exMarkets monitors major market themes and economic developments across currencies, commodities, indices, equities and digital assets, providing research and market intelligence designed to help users understand what is moving markets and why.

How markets are analysed

Understand the forces behind price movements.

FUNDAMENTAL ANALYSIS

Economic indicators, monetary policy, company results, employment data, inflation, interest rates and geopolitical developments can all influence financial markets.

TECHNICAL ANALYSIS

Charts, price structure, trends, support and resistance, momentum and other technical tools can help traders study historical and current market behaviour.

EVENT-DRIVEN ANALYSIS

Major economic releases and scheduled events can create significant changes in volatility and market expectations. Understanding the event, consensus expectations and potential market implications is an important part of event-driven analysis.

Trading discipline

A strategy is only part of the process.

Successful trading requires more than identifying a potential market opportunity. Traders should consider risk, position size, leverage, liquidity, transaction costs and the possibility that markets may move against their expectations.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What markets can I trade?

Financial markets include foreign exchange, commodities, indices, stocks and cryptocurrencies. The markets available to a trader depend on the broker, platform and jurisdiction.

What is fundamental analysis?

Fundamental analysis examines economic, financial and geopolitical factors that may influence the value of an asset or market.

What is technical analysis?

Technical analysis studies price behaviour, market structure, trends and other chart-based information to identify potential market patterns and scenarios.

Why is risk management important?

Risk management helps traders control the amount of capital exposed to individual positions and market movements. It is an important part of maintaining disciplined trading practices.

Does exMarkets provide financial advice?

No. exMarkets provides financial-market research, market information, analysis, commentary and educational content. This information is not intended to constitute personalised financial or investment advice.
